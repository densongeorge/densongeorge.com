_This is an introductory sample post. It is included to demonstrate the publishing setup and can be replaced when the first permanent post is ready._

This site is prepared for straightforward writing in Markdown. Each post has a publication date and can also include a short description and tags.

## A simple structure

Good notes do not need much ceremony. A clear title, a useful idea, and enough context for the reader are often sufficient.

The content collection keeps that structure consistent while leaving the writing itself as plain text in the repository.
